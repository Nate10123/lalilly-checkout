Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information LA LILLY ("we," "us," "our") collects when you visit or shop at lalilly-checkout.pages.dev (the "Site"), and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the information needed to fulfill it: your name, email address, shipping address, and order details. Payment card information is entered directly into our payment processor, Stripe, and never touches our own servers.

If you sign up for our email list, we collect your email address to send you updates about drops, restocks, and promotions. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

We also automatically collect some technical information when you browse the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, through standard web server logs and first-party cookies used to keep your shopping cart working between pages.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill your order, including sharing necessary details (like your shipping address) with the service providers who print, package, and ship your items
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to customer service requests
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in
  • To keep the Site secure and working properly

Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the third-party services required to run the Site and fulfill your order — our payment processor (Stripe), our order fulfillment and shipping providers, and our hosting and email providers. Each of these has its own privacy practices governing the data they process on our behalf.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your cart and session working as you browse. We don't currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Data retention

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, meet legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, reach out to us via the contact links in the site footer.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via Instagram or TikTok.
